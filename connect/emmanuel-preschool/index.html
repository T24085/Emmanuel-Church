Overview

A Christ-Centered Start for Young Learners.

Emmanuel's Preschool is a ministry of Emmanuel Church whose goal is to provide a Christ-centered and academic setting for children ages 3-5 and give them a firm foundation for kindergarten.

Emmanuel's Preschool includes topics from the Old and New Testament, with an emphasis on the truth that we have one God, that Jesus is His son, and that Jesus was sent to be our rescuer.

The program also places equal importance on giving each child the academic and social/emotional skills needed to help them be successful in kindergarten.

Ask the preschool office about class availability. Our team can help with forms, questions, and next steps.

Mission & services

The Preschool Day is Built Around Learning, Formation, and Safety.

All classes are taught in a way that encourages individual and group activities, structured learning, free play, creativity, imagination, social development, and safety.

Christ-centered

Bible Themes Woven Into the Classroom.

The preschool includes topics from the Old and New Testament, with emphasis on one God, Jesus as His son, and Jesus sent as our rescuer.

Whole-child growth

Academic and Social/Emotional Skills Matter Together.

The program gives children the foundation they need for kindergarten while helping them grow in confidence, communication, and relationships.

KDHE Licensed and Standards-Minded.

Admissions

Before You Enroll.

Review the enrollment requirements, then contact our team with any questions.

Open to all

Families from Every Background are Welcome.

The preschool is open to all children regardless of race, color, creed, religion, national origin, ancestry, physical handicap, or sex.

Readiness

Children Must be 3 and Potty-Trained.

Every child must be at least 3 years old prior to attendance and must be potty-trained before starting.

Health records

Forms, Exam, and Immunizations are Required.

Each child must have a medical examination within 6 months of school starting and updated immunization records signed and on file.

Enrollment fee

$25 Nonrefundable Fee Holds the Spot.

Applications are accepted once the required forms are received. Class size is limited to 10 children per class, and other applications are placed on a waiting list.

Program rules

Dismissal Policy Follows the Live Site Copy.

The preschool reserves the right to dismiss a child if they are consistently incompatible with other children or if monthly tuition is not paid on time.
