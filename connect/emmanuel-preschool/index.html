A joyful beginning

A Small School With a Whole-Child View.

Emmanuel Preschool is a ministry of Emmanuel Church, created to give young learners a firm foundation in faith, relationships, and early academics.

Each day balances individual attention and group discovery—circle time, small groups, snack, music and movement, recreation, creativity, and plenty of room to play.

Inside the classroom

The heart of the program

Faith, Learning, and Care Shape Every Day.

Three priorities, held together in one thoughtful classroom rhythm.

01

Faith

Jesus is part of the everyday rhythm.

Old and New Testament stories help children know one God, meet Jesus as His Son, and understand that Jesus came to be our rescuer.

02

Learning

Kindergarten readiness grows through play.

Structured learning, creativity, imagination, movement, and free play build the academic and social-emotional skills children need next.

03

Care

Small classes make room for every child.

A ten-child maximum and KDHE-licensed environment keep safety, relationships, and individual development at the center of the day.

Before you enroll

The Important Details, Kept Simple.

Review the essentials here, then contact our team for current forms, costs, and availability.

01ReadinessAge and Classroom Readiness

Every child must be at least 3 years old before attending and must be potty-trained before starting.

02Health recordsForms, Exams, and Immunizations

Each child must have a medical examination within six months of school starting and updated immunization records signed and on file.

03Fees and class sizeCurrent Fees and Availability

Class size is limited to ten children. Contact the preschool office for current tuition, enrollment fees, and waiting-list availability.

04Open to allEvery Family is Welcome

The preschool is open to all children regardless of race, color, creed, religion, national origin, ancestry, physical handicap, or sex.

05Program policiesProgram Expectations

The preschool reserves the right to dismiss a child if they are consistently incompatible with other children or if monthly tuition is not paid on time.
