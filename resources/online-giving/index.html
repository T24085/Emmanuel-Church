Giving

Generosity is Part of Discipleship.

God is the giver of every good gift. We respond with gratitude, giving through the local church as an act of worship and trust. Your generosity supports the ministry of Emmanuel and helps us share the love of Christ.

Why we give

Generosity is Worship in Motion.

We do not give because God needs something from us. We give because God is accomplishing something through us and within us. As we faithfully steward all He has entrusted to us, our worship deepens, our hearts are transformed, and His Kingdom advances.

  1. 01

    Ownership

    Everything Comes From God.

    Everything we have—our time, talents, treasure, relationships, and opportunities—is a gift from God. Because He is the true owner of all things, we faithfully steward what He has entrusted to us.

    Scripture · KJV

    “Every good gift and every perfect gift is from above, and cometh down from the Father of lights, with whom is no variableness, neither shadow of turning.”

    James 1:17

    “But who am I, and what is my people, that we should be able to offer so willingly after this sort? for all things come of thee, and of thine own have we given thee.”

    1 Chronicles 29:14
  2. 02

    Response

    Giving Is Worship.

    Giving is more than a financial transaction; it is an act of worship. When we give, we honor God, declare that He comes first, and express our trust in His provision.

    Scripture · KJV

    “Honour the LORD with thy substance, and with the firstfruits of all thine increase:”

    Proverbs 3:9

    “I beseech you therefore, brethren, by the mercies of God, that ye present your bodies a living sacrifice, holy, acceptable unto God, which is your reasonable service.”

    Romans 12:1
  3. 03

    Formation

    Generosity Transforms Us.

    Generosity is a spiritual practice that shapes our hearts. As we faithfully give, God grows our faith, loosens our grip on earthly possessions, and forms us into people who reflect His generous character.

    Scripture · KJV

    “And this they did, not as we hoped, but first gave their own selves to the Lord, and unto us by the will of God.”

    2 Corinthians 8:5

    “Therefore, as ye abound in every thing, in faith, and utterance, and knowledge, and in all diligence, and in your love to us, see that ye abound in this grace also.”

    2 Corinthians 8:7
  4. 04

    Mission

    The Kingdom Grows.

    God uses our faithfulness to accomplish far more than we can see. Through our generosity, the Gospel advances, the church is strengthened, lives are changed, and God receives the glory.

    Scripture · KJV

    “But seek ye first the kingdom of God, and his righteousness; and all these things shall be added unto you.”

    Matthew 6:33

    “Being enriched in every thing to all bountifulness, which causeth through us thanksgiving to God.”

    2 Corinthians 9:11

Generosity begins with what God has already given.

Questions?

A few helpful answers.

Find quick answers about online giving, recurring gifts, processing fees, and getting support.

Still need help? Contact the church
How do I give online?+

Select any Give Now button on this page to open Emmanuel's Fellowship One Giving portal. From there, you can make a gift securely online.

Can I make a one-time or recurring gift?+

Yes. Fellowship One Giving supports both one-time gifts and recurring support, so you can choose the rhythm that best fits your generosity journey.

Can I choose whether to cover processing fees?+

Yes. The giving portal provides an option to cover processing fees when you make your gift.

Can I manage my giving online?+

Yes. Fellowship One Giving lets you manage your giving account and review your gifts in one place.

Who do I contact if I need help?+

For help with giving, login access, or receipts, please reach out through the church's contact page and our team will help connect you with the right person.
