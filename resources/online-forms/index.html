Scholarship

Wilma & Lester Green Scholarship Form

For applicants who reside in Dickinson County, Kansas, with preference for youth active in a Dickinson County church or newspaper carriers from The Abilene Reflector Chronicle. Applicants must attend an eligible Kansas college, vocational, or community college and show financial need.
